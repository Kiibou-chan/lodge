title: "Provenance is exact only where it must be"
---

Summarised reasoning is enough as provenance.
Exact provenance is kept for the author's own words, as a quote; for numbers, as a record or a runnable command; and for what a decision replaces or narrows, as an ID.
Provenance is kept in the repository, not in transcripts.
