title: "Knowledge is read at three levels"
---

Knowledge is read at three levels.
Level 1 is the decision or specification entry: title, statement, status, who decided, date, scope, and any review flag; every read returns it by default, and the author browses it.
Level 2 is the reasoning, read only when asked for by name.
Level 3 is provenance: the author's quote, number handles, what the decision replaces or narrows, the work item, and attachments.
