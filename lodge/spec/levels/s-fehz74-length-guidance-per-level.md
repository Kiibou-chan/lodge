Length guidance is soft, set in project configuration, and tuned in the pilot.
A title is one line, with feedback above 100 characters.
A decision statement is up to three sentences, with feedback above 80 words.
A specification statement is one paragraph, with feedback above 150 words.
Reasoning runs to a few hundred words, with feedback above 1,000 words.
