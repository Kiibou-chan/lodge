title: "Search and show give Level 1 first"
---

Search covers titles, statements, and codes, with filters by kind, scope, status, area, and decided-by, and its results are Level 1 lines.
With `--brief`, search gives one line per record instead, its header and title without the statement.
Showing a record gives Level 1, and `show` takes several refs, printed from one read with each record once and a line naming each ref after the first; a coded record's sibling codes and the records citing its code follow as one brief line each; reasoning and provenance come only when asked for by name.
