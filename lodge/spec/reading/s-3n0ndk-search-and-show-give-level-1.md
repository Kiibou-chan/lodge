title: "Search and show give Level 1 first"
---

Search covers titles, statements, and codes, with filters by kind, scope, status, area, and decided-by, and its results are Level 1 lines.
With `--brief`, search gives one line per record instead, its header and title without the statement.
Showing one record gives Level 1; reasoning and provenance come only when asked for by name.
