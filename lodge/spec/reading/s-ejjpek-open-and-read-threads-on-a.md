title: "Open and read threads on a record"
---

Every decision and entry page offers "Raise a doubt" and "Ask a question", each with a text field, and shows its threads newest last.
An open thread has a reply field and a "Close" button.
`lodge threads` lists open threads, and `lodge thread q-xxxxxx` shows one.
Search and the timeline cover decisions and entries; threads are read through these commands and their record's page.
