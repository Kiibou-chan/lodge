title: "Where record files live in a project"
---

In the project's repository, an area lives at `lodge/spec/<area>/_area.md`, holding its title, one-line intro, and ordered entry IDs.
Specification entries, current and retired, live at `lodge/spec/<area>/s-xxxxxx-<slug>.md`.
Decisions, current and replaced, live at `lodge/decisions/d-xxxxxx-<slug>.md`.
Expanded evidence lives under `lodge/attachments/<id>/`, outside the default read path.
