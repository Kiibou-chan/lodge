title: "Kinds of knowledge and projects outside the scope"
---

Out of scope for now are the phone, other kinds of project knowledge beyond decisions and specification entries, the author's personal to-do list, conversation logs as provenance, and porting a second project onto lodge.
What other knowledge might be useful has to be evaluated first on the past and then in a pilot.
The to-do list may be integrated later in whatever way makes sense, and whether a second project adopts lodge stays open.
