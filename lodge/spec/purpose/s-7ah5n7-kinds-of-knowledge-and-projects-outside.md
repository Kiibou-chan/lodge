title: "Kinds of knowledge and projects outside the scope"
---

For lodge 0.2, out of scope for now are the phone, other kinds of project knowledge beyond decisions and specification entries, the author's personal to-do list, conversation logs as provenance, and porting a second project onto lodge.
A thread, a doubt or question on a decision or entry with its messages, holds no claim of its own; what it settles enters lodge as a decision.
For lodge 0.2, other kinds of knowledge come into scope only after an evaluation against past records and then a pilot.
On the server, tasks, artefacts, and session reporting are in scope from build step 2, the phone interface from build step 3, and use by several people from build step 7.
