title: "Cost and speed measures for the pilot"
---

After the pilot, three measures concern cost and speed.
Advisor rulings are cheaper and quicker than the baseline measured on the first user, in fresh tokens, calls, and minutes per ruling, and the author can see the difference herself.
Upkeep tokens stay below the reading tokens saved.
Finding a decision in the viewer is quicker than asking a session.
