title: "lodge must not show wrong information"
---

lodge must not show wrong information, including a short entry that reads as more settled than it is.
Every Level 1 view carries who decided, the status, and any open review flag.
Reads and the viewer use the same parser and check as the gate, so a record that fails the check is shown as failing.
Derived facts, such as reverse links, review flags, statuses, and what replaced what, are computed and never typed by hand.
