title: "Correctness and use measures for the pilot"
---

After the pilot, three further measures concern correctness and use.
Entries are correct on a random sample checked against their sources.
Every change names what it changes; the baseline is that few entries in the first user's logs say what they replace.
Agents actually use lodge, so that no decision is written anywhere else; the author named this the most important measure.
