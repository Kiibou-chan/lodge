Evaluation on the first user's past happens in a scratch copy.
A sample of decisions, rulings, and specification sections is converted through lodge, past advisor rulings are replayed with and without it, and the success measures are measured.
The sample size and replay method go in their own plan once lodge exists, since they set most of the cost, and that plan also owns the importer and all measurement.
