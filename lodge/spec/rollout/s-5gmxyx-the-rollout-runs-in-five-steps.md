title: "The rollout runs in five steps"
---

The rollout runs in an agreed order.
First, lodge is built, test-first.
Second, lodge keeps its own decisions in lodge, starting with those of the brainstorm that designed it.
Third, lodge is evaluated on the first user's past in a scratch copy.
Fourth, a pilot: the first user's new decisions go only into lodge, with the old files frozen.
Fifth, the old files are replaced once the pilot holds.
