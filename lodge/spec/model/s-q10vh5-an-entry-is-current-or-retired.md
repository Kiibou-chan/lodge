title: "An entry is current or retired, derived from decisions"
---

An entry's status is current or retired, and is derived rather than stored.
An entry is retired while a decision that lists it in `changes` as retired is not replaced (LODGE-7).
Its file stays where it is.
Replacing the retiring decision makes the entry current again, back in its area's order.
