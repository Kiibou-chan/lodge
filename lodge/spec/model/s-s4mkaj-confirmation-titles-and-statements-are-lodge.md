title: "Confirmation titles and statements are lodge text"
rests_on: [s-bhew59]
---

For a single decision, the generated title is "Confirms d-xxxxxx" and the statement is "The author confirms d-xxxxxx.".
For an area, the title is "Confirms the decisions behind area NAME" and the statement lists the confirmed decisions.
These generated fields are lodge text; only the quote and reasoning contain the author's words.
