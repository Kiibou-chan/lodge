title: "Author messages retain verbatim words and provenance"
---

An author message contains only verbatim words typed in the viewer or quoted from a session.
A command-line author message requires `--where` and `--when`; an agent uses `--by author` only for her verbatim words from that source.
The viewer records `author`, `where: "lodge viewer"`, and the write time to the minute as `%Y-%m-%d %H:%M`.
