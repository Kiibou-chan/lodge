title: "A decision records a change, with a scope"
---

A decision, with the ID prefix `d-`, records a change, close to an architecture decision record.
Its title and statement say what was decided, at Level 1, and its reasoning is the body under a heading, at Level 2.
Its scope is `product` or `process`: one record kind with a scope field, so the first user's decisions and rulings become one kind, and a process ruling is a decision with scope `process` that nothing else sets apart.
