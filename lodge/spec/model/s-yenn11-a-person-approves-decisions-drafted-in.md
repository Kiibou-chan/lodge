title: "A person approves decisions drafted in their name"
rests_on: [s-ava922, s-pks1qn]
---

A person's decision is written only through that person.
A session drafts it with the person's quote, and the draft waits for that person's approval; until approval it is not a current decision.
From build step 2, it appears in that person's cross-project waiting list.
