title: "Acting on a thread requires a decision"
rests_on: [s-01k42c]
---

An agent correcting or retiring a record or its dependants in response to a thread writes a decision naming that thread in its reasoning and appends a reply describing the action and naming the decision.
The agent closes it only when the author's last message requested closure or the author closes it herself.
Who works out the appropriate response remains open between author and agents.
An agent change from a thread on an author record raises the usual review flag.
