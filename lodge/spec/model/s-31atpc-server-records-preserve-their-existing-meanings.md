title: "Server records preserve their existing meanings"
rests_on: [s-z1zvb4]
---

Specification entries, decisions, areas, threads of doubts and questions, confirmations, levels, review flags, codes, and links keep the meanings of the first design and the viewer design.
Decisions remain immutable, and changes to specification entries retain their history in append-only events.
