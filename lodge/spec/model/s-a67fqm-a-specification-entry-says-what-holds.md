title: "A specification entry says what holds now"
---

lodge keeps two kinds of record, specification entries and decisions.
A specification entry, with the ID prefix `s-`, says what holds now, at Level 1, and is rewritten in place.
It has an area, a title, and a statement.
It has no reasoning of its own: its reasoning is the decisions that changed it, which lodge lists newest first.
