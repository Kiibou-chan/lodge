title: "A decision is current or replaced, derived from later decisions"
---

A decision's status is current or replaced, and is derived: it is replaced when a later decision names it in `replaces`.
Nothing in the replaced decision's file changes, and the file does not move.
Reads and the viewer show current records by default and the rest by filter.
A narrowed decision stays current and shows what narrows it.
