Three questions stay open: the licence, whether a second project adopts lodge, and the values of the length guidance.

## Reasoning

The licence is the author's to choose before others are invited to use lodge.

Whether a second project later adopts lodge matters only then, because its ID prefixes may clash with lodge's.

The length guidance values are tuned in the pilot.
