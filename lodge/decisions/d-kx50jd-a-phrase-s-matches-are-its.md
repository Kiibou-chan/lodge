d-emtwbs holds for what a phrase matches: a record whose title or statement holds the phrase's words as a contiguous run; the records d-t2dsd3 lists below those, holding every word in another order or some of the words, are near matches, not matches of the phrase.

## Reasoning

d-t2dsd3 made a phrase list near matches below its exact runs, which left d-emtwbs current with a statement that read as if only runs were listed; the Task 8b review's D1 named the gap.
This keeps d-emtwbs's rule for what a phrase matches and says where the other records stand, so the two decisions read together without contradiction.
