Length guidance is soft and set in project configuration: a title of one line, a decision statement of up to three sentences, a specification statement of one paragraph, and reasoning of a few hundred words.

## Reasoning

The specification author chose the values as guidance to be tuned in the pilot, and feedback begins above the figures given, which are the values in the table at the line cited for each.

For scale, the first user's longest records run to hundreds of words, all at one level today.

The limits are soft because the author doubts that hard limits would serve writers well, and guardrails alone usually already help.
