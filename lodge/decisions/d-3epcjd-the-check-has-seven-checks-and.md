The check gates publication, as a check before merge, and has seven checks.
They cover the header, identifiers, the author's quote, entries without decisions, altered decisions, new links to superseded records, and entry order.

## Reasoning

The check before merge was agreed with the author.

The seven are: a header that cannot be read, an identifier that points nowhere or is shared, an author attribution without her words, a specification change with no decision behind it, a decision altered after it was made, a new link to something already superseded, and an entry outside its area's order.

Decisions are immutable, which is why a correction or a shorter reasoning is a new decision that replaces the old one (LODGE-10).

Older records keep their links to superseded records, and reads show each target's status beside them, so history is not rewritten to satisfy the sixth check.

Imported records are exempt from the author-quote and entry-needs-decision checks, as the rollout area describes.
