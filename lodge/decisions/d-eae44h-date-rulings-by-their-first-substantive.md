I2, a ruling is dated by the first commit whose ledger line holds it with the same substance; a later edit that only renames a word (line 187, "handoff" to "brainstorm" in the redaction commit) does not re-date it.

## Reasoning

The date says when the ruling was made, and the redaction made no ruling.
Cost if wrong: line 187 sorts before her answer to D13, which it preceded in fact.
