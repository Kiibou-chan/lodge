All writing goes through lodge, a decision and the entries it changes together, with feedback at write time and never a refusal. The feedback covers length, related decisions, numbers without a handle, and a coming review flag.

## Reasoning

Writing through lodge, never by hand, is how every change to the specification comes to name the decision behind it.

Feedback rather than refusal at write time was settled with the author as the guardrail style.

The question about related decisions exists because the baseline shows few entries say what they replace, so the feedback asks when a decision that changes nothing, replaces nothing, and narrows nothing sits among related current decisions.

The check on digits without a `numbers` handle was the specification author's call, so that figures can be traced.

The note that an agent decision will raise a review flag lets the writer act on it before publishing, since a published decision is immutable.
