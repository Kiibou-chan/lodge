Confirming a record does not show it as written by the author, and who wrote what stays visible.

## Reasoning

On confirmation, I believe it would not be good if everything I read and confirmed is then marked as mine. It's still very important to know who actually wrote what. Just because I read and confirmed it does not mean that I understand it fully or all of its consequences.
