Provenance is kept in the repository, because cloud transcripts are not always available.
Exact provenance is required only for the author's own words as a quote, for numbers as a record or runnable command, and for what a decision replaces or narrows as an ID.
Expanded evidence such as logs can be attached for evaluation, outside the default read path.

## Reasoning

Provenance has to be accessible, cloud transcripts are not always for example.
